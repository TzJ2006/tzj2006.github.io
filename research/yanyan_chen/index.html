Breakthrough Work#

1. Shale Gas Formation and Occurrence in China: An Overview of the Current Status and Future Potential (2016)#

Description: A systematic review of the geological origins, reservoir characteristics, and development potential of shale gas in China, cited 56 times. It is the most cited single paper in the profile and serves as an important review in the fields of Chinese shale gas policy and resource assessment.

Why it couldn’t be done before: Before 2016, there was insufficient data accumulation on shale gas exploration in China, and systematic geological evaluations in regions such as the Sichuan Basin were still in early stages. Meanwhile, the shale gas revolution in the United States provided a technical framework that made comparative studies possible.

Impact: It provided a systematic reference for domestic shale gas resource assessment and supported the planning discussions on shale gas development pathways at the energy policy level.

2. Plant species diversity and dynamics in forests invaded by Moso bamboo (Phyllostachys edulis) in Tianmu Mountain Nature Reserve (2013)#

Description: Studies the impact of bamboo expansion on forest plant diversity in Tianmu Mountain Nature Reserve, cited 53 times. It is an important empirical study in bioinvasion ecology regarding the mechanism of bamboo spread.

Why it couldn’t be done before: Long-term monitoring data from targeted sites were required, and the long-term vegetation monitoring system in Tianmu Mountain was only improved in the late 2000s. At the same time, the ecological threat of bamboo expansion attracted systematic attention from the domestic ecology community in the early 2010s.

Impact: It provided ecological evidence for Chinese subtropical forest protection and bamboo management policies, and promoted research on the impact of bamboo expansion on biodiversity.

3. Heat transfer characteristics of oscillating flow regenerator filled with circular tubes or parallel plates (2007)#

Description: Studies the heat transfer properties of an oscillating flow regenerator (a core component of thermoacoustic systems) in thermoacoustic cooling and cryogenic engineering, cited 35 times. It is a fundamental experimental study in these fields.

Why it couldn’t be done before: The mechanism of oscillating flow heat transfer is complex, and early measurement instruments were not precise enough to distinguish the heat transfer differences between circular tubes and parallel plates. The maturity of thermoacoustic system experimental platforms in the early 2000s made precise parametric studies possible.

Impact: It provided experimental basis for the optimized design of thermoacoustic refrigerators and thermoacoustic engines. Subsequent papers on thermoacoustic engines (cited 27–30 times) between 2014–2015 were based on this work.

4. Study of a liquid-piston traveling-wave thermoacoustic heat engine with different working gases (2014)#

Description: Studies the performance of a traveling-wave thermoacoustic engine using different working gases, cited 30 times. It represents a systematic experimental exploration in thermoacoustic energy conversion using a liquid piston configuration.

Why it couldn’t be done before: Sealing and dynamic stability issues in liquid piston thermoacoustic systems were difficult to solve engineering-wise in the early days. The theory of traveling-wave thermoacoustics became mature after Backhaus & Swift (1999), and experimental verification work emerged in large numbers in the 2010s.

Impact: It promoted the transition of thermoacoustic engines from laboratory concepts to engineering applications, especially in the potential application of low-grade waste heat recovery.

5. An Evaluating Method of Public Transit Accessibility for Urban Areas Based on GIS (2016)#

Description: Proposes a regional public transit accessibility evaluation method based on GIS (APTA concept), cited 21 times. It quantifies and integrates passenger behavior psychology and service scope into urban transportation planning.

Why it couldn’t be done before: Large-scale GIS spatial analysis was costly before the 2010s. The popularity of mobile internet and bus IC card data made behavior parameter calibration possible, transforming accessibility models from theory to practicality.

Impact: It provides a methodological tool for quantitative evaluation of urban public transportation planning and has certain reference value in the domestic urban transportation planning community.
