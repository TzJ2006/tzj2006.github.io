Breakthrough Work#

1. Shale Gas Formation and Occurrence in China: An Overview of the Current Status and Future Potential (2016)#

Description: A systematic review of the geological origin, reservoir characteristics, and development potential of shale gas in China, with 56 citations, making it the most cited single paper in the profile. It is an important review in the fields of Chinese shale gas policy and resource assessment.

Why it couldn’t be done before: Before 2016, there was insufficient data accumulation on shale gas exploration in China, and systematic geological evaluation in regions such as the Sichuan Basin was still in its early stages; meanwhile, the U.S. shale gas revolution provided a technical framework that made comparative studies possible.

Impact: It provided a systematic reference for domestic shale gas resource assessment and supported the planning discussions on shale gas development paths at the energy policy level.

2. Plant species diversity and dynamics in forests invaded by Moso bamboo (Phyllostachys edulis) in Tianmu Mountain Nature Reserve (2013)#

Description: Studies the impact of bamboo expansion on forest plant diversity in Tianmu Mountain Nature Reserve, with 53 citations, and is an important empirical study in ecological invasion ecology regarding bamboo spread mechanisms.

Why it couldn’t be done before: Long-term monitoring data from targeted sites were needed, and the long-term vegetation monitoring system in Tianmu Mountain was only improved in the late 2000s; at the same time, the ecological threat of bamboo expansion attracted systematic attention in domestic ecology in the early 2010s.

Impact: It provided ecological evidence for Chinese subtropical forest protection and bamboo management policies, and promoted research topics on the impact of bamboo expansion on biodiversity.

3. Heat transfer characteristics of oscillating flow regenerator filled with circular tubes or parallel plates (2007)#

Description: Studies the heat transfer properties of an oscillating flow regenerator (a core component of thermoacoustic systems) with 35 citations, and is a fundamental experimental study in thermoacoustic cooling and cryogenic engineering.

Why it couldn’t be done before: The mechanism of oscillating flow heat transfer is complex, and early measurement instruments were not precise enough to distinguish the heat transfer differences between circular tubes and parallel plates; the maturity of experimental platforms for thermoacoustic systems in the early 2000s made precise parameterized studies possible.

Impact: It provided experimental basis for the optimized design of thermoacoustic coolers and thermoacoustic engines. Subsequent papers on thermoacoustic engines (cited 27–30 times) between 2014–2015 were based on this work.

4. Study of a liquid-piston traveling-wave thermoacoustic heat engine with different working gases (2014)#

Description: Studies the performance of a traveling-wave thermoacoustic engine using different working gas substances with 30 citations, representing a systematic experimental exploration in thermoacoustic energy conversion using a liquid piston configuration.

Why it couldn’t be done before: Sealing and dynamic stability issues in liquid piston thermoacoustic systems were difficult to solve engineering-wise early on; the theory of traveling-wave thermoacoustics gradually matured after Backhaus & Swift (1999), and experimental verification work emerged extensively in the 2010s.

Impact: It promoted the transition of thermoacoustic engines from laboratory concepts to engineering applications, especially in the potential application field of low-grade waste heat recovery.

5. An Evaluating Method of Public Transit Accessibility for Urban Areas Based on GIS (2016)#

Description: Proposes a GIS-based method for evaluating urban public transit accessibility (APTA concept), with 21 citations, integrating passenger behavior psychology and service scope into urban transportation planning.

Why it couldn’t be done before: Large-scale GIS spatial analysis was costly before the 2010s; the popularization of mobile internet and bus IC card data made it possible to calibrate behavior parameters, transforming accessibility models from theory to operability.

Impact: It provided methodological tools for quantitative evaluation of urban public transportation planning and has certain reference value in domestic urban transportation planning academia.
