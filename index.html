01 / OUR APPROACH

More autonomy.
Less guesswork.

A harness is the working system around your AI: capturing intent, guiding execution, and retaining verification. We explore how it helps people understand and direct agent work.

01 ↗

Start with intent

Turn a goal into reviewable ideas. Make the purpose, approach, dependencies, and expected outcome explicit before implementation.

02 ↗

Make “done” inspectable

Connect an idea to its code and verification results. Inspect the record of a real check, beyond an agent’s completion message.

03 ↗

Keep the gaps visible

Separate work in progress, untouched tasks, and blockers. Follow the dependencies and decide what you or your AI should tackle next.
