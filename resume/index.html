Representative Research & Papers#

Robotics / Embodied AI#

FLARE: A Failure-Aware Framework for Autonomous Correction and Recovery in Vision-Language-Action Models G. Zhao, Z. Tang, X. Chen, Z. Kuang, Y. Tian, G. Li | CVPR 2026

  • Proposed the Retry-and-Reset recovery framework, enabling VLA policies to perform self-correction after disturbances, including external interferences such as policy failures and human interventions.
  • Addressed the “fragility” issue of VLA models—where minor perturbations lead to catastrophic failures—thereby enhancing the robustness of VLA models.
  • Achieved an 84.0% success rate across 9 contact-intensive tasks in RoboMimic, representing a 26.2% improvement over previous state-of-the-art methods.

RecoverBench: A Benchmark for Error Recovery in Robotic Manipulation Z. Tang, G. Zhao, X. Chen, et al., G. Li | Under Review, 2026

  • Constructed a reproducible robot error recovery benchmark, containing 12 error skills, 136 error subtypes, and 1,360 validated MuJoCo post-error scenarios across 6 manipulation tasks.
  • Designed an automated error injection mechanism to systematically introduce failures during manipulation, achieving a standardized and comprehensive evaluation of robot error recovery.
  • Collected and augmented 1,641 human recovery demonstrations, converted into 10,598 recovery trajectories, supporting downstream recovery policy learning and evaluation.

Latent Bridge: Accelerating Dual-System VLA Models via Latent Feature Prediction Y. Liu, Y. Li, Z. Tang, et al., Y. Chen, H. Li | arXiv, 2026

  • Developed a latent bridging method that reduces VLM backbone calls by efficiently predicting visual features while maintaining task success rates.
  • Achieved up to a 75% reduction in VLM calls, bringing a 1.73x speedup on LIBERO and RoboCasa.
  • Validated universality across different VLA policies (including GR00T and $\pi$ series models), demonstrating a generalizable VLM robot control acceleration plugin method.
