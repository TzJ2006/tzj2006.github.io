Research & projects

1 min

I study how robots can act reliably under changing conditions and how vision-language-action models can run more efficiently. Selected research is outlined below; see my résumé for experience and author information.

RecoverBench#

Evaluating error recovery in robotic manipulation through reproducible failure scenarios and recovery demonstrations.

FLARE#

Failure-aware autonomous correction and recovery for vision-language-action models, exploring retry and reset after disturbances.

Latent Bridge#

Reducing vision-language backbone calls through latent feature prediction for efficient dual-system VLA inference.

AI for Science#

Research on single-cell drug response, perturbation prediction and medical image analysis, including scDrugMap, scPerb and PINet.
