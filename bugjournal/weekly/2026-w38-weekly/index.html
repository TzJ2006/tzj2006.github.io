Learnings#

Architecture (architecture)#

  • For VLA models, W4A8 is the ‘Sweet Spot’: weight 4-bit is highly tolerable, while A4 activation causes catastrophic precision collapse in the backbone.
  • Visual prompts must prioritize ’training-robustness’ (surviving crop/rotate) and ‘policy-utility’ (scene-anchored) over aesthetic clarity.

Debugging (debugging)#

  • Zero-standard deviation in a training subset can cause catastrophic failure in multi-task normalization statistics.
  • In high-concurrency environments, worker limits must account for ‘grandchild’ processes; optimization follows a U-shaped curve.

Tools (tools)#

  • When fork history is rewritten, ‘git merge’ is often unusable; cherry-picking is a more reliable reconstruction method.

Domain Knowledge (domain)#
