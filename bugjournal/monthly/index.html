Monthly Summary 2026-04

The first 7 days of this month (April 1–7) saw four work streams advancing in high-intensity parallel: the TokenMonitor Linux cross-platform FloatBall bug was resolved through 10 iterations over 5 days via an architectural rewrite (fixed window + GDK Input Shape); the MIHD multimodal spatial transcriptomics paper moved from experiment wrap-up to a bilingual draft, with the discovery of the clustering-retrieval tradeoff as a core new argument; Life-Copilot completed a large-scale Discord Bot refactor (net -2,823 lines) and shipped Screen Time Tracker and other new capabilities; Gadget infrastructure completed initialization of a new Linux workstation, bidirectional data sync, and website deployment pipeline repair. The defining pattern throughout the month: low-level platform constraints (Linux GTK/WM/Wayland) repeatedly invalidated AI’s first-round assumptions, and humans drove correct fix paths by insisting on symptom description and demanding root-cause analysis; Critic subagent adversarial review proactively surfaced high-value risks — Wayland compatibility, lark-oapi blocking, and others — across multiple features.
