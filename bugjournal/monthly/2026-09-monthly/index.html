Monthly Learnings Digest#

Architecture (architecture)#

  • Hierarchical ‘container’ patterns and per-item content-bound approvals prevent user ‘approval fatigue’. Symmetric aggregation (Deep Sets) is superior to one-on encoding for unordered sets in RL. A ‘Four-Quadrant’ classification (Who moves x When it moves) is ideal for VLM memory tasks. (Source: 2026-09-05, 2026-09-07, 2026-09-08, 2026-09-12)

Debugging (debugging)#

  • Successful compilation does not guarantee low-precision path usage; always audit for silent float fallbacks. Low FPS/low utilization often indicates display driver/DWM bottlenecks. When Git history is rewritten, cherry-picking is more reliable than ‘git merge’. (Source: 2026-09-01, 2026-09-07, 2026-09-14, 2026-09-17)

Domain Knowledge (domain)#

  • In robotics/VLM, geometric precision is as critical as topological correctness. In quantization, W4A8 is often the ‘sweet spot’; 4-bit activation (A4) in sensitive layers like RMSNorm causes catastrophic drift. Spatial omics fusion benefits are non-monotonic across granularities. (Source: 2026-09-06, 2026-09-14, 2026-09-17, 2026-09-18, 2026-09-21)

Tools (tools)#

  • Automated CDP clicks are indistinguishable from humans (isTrusted: true). In large-scale migrations, parallelization by name-prefix is more efficient than directory rsync. In high-concurrency environments, worker limits must account for ‘grandchild’ processes to prevent OOM. (Source: 2026-09-05, 2026-09-12, 2026-09-15)
