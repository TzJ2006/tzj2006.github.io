2. RoboMemory training loss was astronomically high (2e11) due to division by zero in normalization.#

Solution: Identified that norm_stats were based on a subset where the 8th dimension (gripper) had zero std; re-computed stats on the full 16-task dataset.

Solution: Abandoned merge in favor of cherry-picking the 10 specific commits made since v0.14.2 onto the upstream branch.

Learnings#

Key Learnings#

  • For VLA models, W4A8 is the ‘Sweet Spot’: weight 4-bit is highly tolerable with minimal performance loss, whereas A4 activation causes catastrophic precision collapse in the backbone.
  • Always ensure normalization statistics are computed on the full intended dataset; zero-std dimensions in a subset will cause catastrophic training failure in multi-task settings.
  • When fork history is rewritten, ‘git merge’ is often unusable due to constant ‘add/add’ conflicts; cherry-picking specific commits is a more reliable reconstruction method.
  • PhD admission in top-tier robotics programs is heavily influenced by ‘prior connections’ (e.g., internal Master’s programs) and direct faculty contact rather than just GPA/publications.
  • Protobuf-JSON serialization often omits empty arrays entirely, which can break parsers expecting specific field structures.
  • Small differences in npm versions between local and CI can cause catastrophic ’npm ci’ failures due to lockfile format drift.
