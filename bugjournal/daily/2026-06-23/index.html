Problems & Solutions#

Critical Issues#

1. GeneJEPA embeddings failed to cluster spatial domains due to high anisotropy and library-size bias, despite complex architecture.#

Solution: Performed SVD and correlation diagnostics; confirmed post-processing (whitening) could not recover signal. Validated that foundational models on single-cell data do not transfer to spatial spot-level clustering.

Key Insight: Complex foundation models may underperform simple linear projections (PCA) in specific small-sample domains due to domain shift and lack of spatial priors.

2. Infrastructure bottlenecks: broken local proxy stalled HuggingFace CLI downloads; dependency mismatches broke RoboCasa environment on high-performance cluster.#

Solution: Bypassed proxies via direct local HTTP downloads and SSH/SCP transfers; pinned specific numpy/mujoco versions and patched config files for stability.

Key Insight: Large binary transfers are more reliable via direct protocols than tunnels; open-source robotics environments often require explicit version pinning and manual patching.

3. Evaluation fairness compromised by prompt design biases in VLMs and inconsistent model initialization across policy tasks.#

Solution: Redesigned Cosmos tests with standardized rubrics for apples-to-apples scoring; reconfigured Pi0.5 training to cold-start from base weights, ensuring differences are attributable to data quality rather than initialization artifacts.

Key Insight: Rigorous experimental design requires controlling for prompt variance in VLMs and identical initialization seeds in policy learning to isolate causal factors.

4. OMEN Gaming Hub software causes system crashes when controlling RGB lights on HP OMEN 45L. Initial AI assumption of software-only solution ignored user’s experiential constraint.#

Solution: Pivoted to hardware-level troubleshooting; provided step-by-step guide to physically disconnect the lighting control board power cable, offering a stable, resource-free alternative.

Key Insight: When OEM software is unstable, verifying hardware architecture allows for reliable manual interventions; user constraints should prioritize avoiding known failure points immediately.

Learnings#

Key Learnings#
