AI Limitations#

Critical Limitations#

  • JEV is a ‘state switch’ rather than a ‘drafter’; it cannot inherently ‘understand and redraw’ environmental changes on its own.

General Limitations#

  • Difficulty in distinguishing between specific VLA ’language degradation’ and ‘observation space shift’ without explicit user guidance.
  • Struggled with permission restrictions on remote servers and encountered syntax errors in complex Node.js text manipulation.
  • Failed to respect hardware constraints (e.g., attempting to move ASR to GPU instead of CPU) and lacked the ability to verify very recent 2026 literature without direct links.

Learnings#

Key Learnings#

  • A unified research narrative (Failure $\rightarrow$ Awareness $\rightarrow$ Memory) is significantly more effective for academic positioning than listing isolated projects.
  • The ’latency gap’ is the core value proposition for JEV: non-pausing agents fail in counting tasks because the robot moves faster than the VLM can respond.
  • Effective robot memory relies on recognizing specific ‘completion events’ (e.g., gripper release) rather than just continuous observation.
  • In VLA training, the lack of ‘recovery’ or ‘stop’ actions in datasets is a primary driver of policy timeouts during OOD failures.
  • Using ’timeout’ mechanisms is a valid design trade-off to ensure system continuity/robustness even when visual guidance fails.
