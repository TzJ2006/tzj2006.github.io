Learnings#

Key Learnings#

  • 4-bit activations for attention and normalization layers are catastrophic without advanced range-optimization; single-component improvements do not compose end-to-end.
  • Server-side deterministic item keys are mandatory in distributed or retry-prone systems to prevent duplicate side effects (e.g., calendar events).
  • When working in highly constrained/guarded environments, a staging/scratchpad workflow is more efficient than attempting direct edits.
  • Validating temporal awareness in vision models requires control sets where frames are identical but sequences differ.
