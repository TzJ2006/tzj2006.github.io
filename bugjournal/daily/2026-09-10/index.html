Critical Issues#

1. RL agent fails to clear ‘ultra-hard’ levels (e.g., level 20) even with full information.#

2. Video reconstruction errors due to fast clicking and tray animation overlapping.#

Solution: Implemented ‘record-only fast path’ and added logic to handle tray jumps and identify missing tiles via stack depth analysis.
