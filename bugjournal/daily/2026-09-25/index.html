Problems & Solutions#

Critical Issues#

1. High idle energy due to hidden popover rendering at 60fps.#

Solution: Introduced popover visibility tracking. Rust emits events to the frontend; CSS pauses animations, and Svelte components pause intervals when hidden.

2. Slow tray updates caused by re-parsing massive log history.#

Solution: Implemented earliest-date caching per provider to ensure only new log files are parsed during incremental updates.

3. Gemini 无法识别计数突变（如 SwingXtimes 错过目标点）#

Solution: 引入本体感知（robot_state）检测关节/夹爪状态变化，并将其转换为视觉徽标（Badge）的触发信号，实现『计数由代码持有，Gemini 只判增量』。

4. 恒存类任务中的目标切换时机错误#

Solution: 通过 writer_probe 诊断出目标句解析滞后，拟通过增加『目标进入/离开检测』的视觉门控来校准。

General Issues#

5. Startup latency from external font requests.#

Solution: Switched to system-ui fonts to remove network dependency during initial render.
