Learnings#

Key Learnings#

  • In camera-ready revisions, it is safer to use ‘Changes since submission’ as a formal section rather than attempting to frame corrections as ‘improvements’.
  • Effective academic networking is about presenting a well-defined research problem that invites others to contribute their expertise, not just requesting favors.
  • The ’non-pause’ (continuous) robot execution mode significantly drops success rates compared to ‘pause’ mode, highlighting the need for fast, high-accuracy local judges (JEV).
  • Rotation (SpinQuant) effectively reduces VE outlier magnitude but does not solve the cumulative error of 4-bit per-tensor weight quantization in deep layers.
  • Gemini 3.1 Pro’s ’thinking tokens’ significantly increase actual proxy costs compared to the launcher’s raw token estimates.

Practical Learnings#

  • Path canonicalization (e.g., /private/var on macOS or 8.3 names on Windows) is essential for cross-platform grouping logic in project management tools.
