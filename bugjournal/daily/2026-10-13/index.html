Learnings#

Key Learnings#

  • A rigorous ‘constraint-based’ narrative in research must clearly distinguish between the benefit of specific inputs (e.g., drawn glyphs) and the benefit of the constraints themselves.
  • Maintaining a ‘source of truth’ link between rebuttal promises, research data, and the actual codebase is critical for successful camera-ready transitions.
  • Directly reading the WeChat message database is significantly more reliable for verifying message history than attempting to infer context from the UI.
  • When integrating complex automation, a ‘receive-only’ phase is essential to prevent ‘runaway’ bots from sending incorrect or duplicate messages.
  • Switching models requires adjusting the entire pre-processing and tool-use logic to match the model’s native strengths, not just changing the ‘brain’.
