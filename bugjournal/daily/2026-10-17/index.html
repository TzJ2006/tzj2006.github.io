Learnings#

Key Learnings#

  • Concat-based fusion is highly susceptible to ‘modality dominance’ from high-variance modalities (like images), even if they are uninformative for specific tasks.
  • In robot learning, strategic ‘division of labor’ (VLM for reasoning, Code for execution) can bridge the gap between SOTA and ground-truth performance.
  • SimpleMemVLA’s success stems from utilizing pre-trained VLM video-native capabilities and a text-based memory bridging mechanism rather than new architecture.
  • Reliable automation requires a multi-layer ‘guard’ system (UI prep, dispatch, and execution) and clear distinction between data-level receipt and UI-level response.
  • A two-layer configuration (Global Mode + Individual Toggle) is essential for preventing unintended actions in complex message-handling systems.
