Problems & Solutions#

Critical Issues#

1. W4A4 quantization of $\pi$0.5 VLA resulted in 0% success in ’libero_goal’ due to quantization noise in RMSNorm outputs exceeding the required action distinction distance.#

Solution: Replaced min-max calibration with TF-Enhanced range search to optimize the 16 quantization levels for 35 RMSNorm outputs.

2. The central claim that fusion improves performance appeared to invert at cell-lineage (GT-B) granularity, potentially undermining the paper’s core hypothesis.#

Solution: Conducted a detailed audit showing that under the fixed protocol, fusion benefits are scale-dependent and providing spatial context at the tissue level may introduce noise at the finer cell-lineage level.

Key Insight: The benefit of NCL fusion is highly dependent on the granularity of the ground truth.

3. Potential setting mismatch if $\pi$0.5 is trained per-task instead of multi-task.#

Solution: Verified remote training scripts and confirmed official baseline uses multi-task training; adjusted local pipeline to match.

4. SSH sync resulted in inconsistent usage totals across devices due to duplicate records and mismatched provider tags.#

Solution: Implemented message-ID-based deduplication in the extractor and a re-tagging mechanism in the archive layer to collapse records into the ‘all’ provider bucket.

5. Hugo build failed because the translator wrote raw LaTeX math ($\pi$) into YAML frontmatter without escaping.#

Solution: Modified frontmatter.py to decode values with json.loads and re-encode with json.dumps during writing.

6. Discrepancy in DLPFC clustering results (STAIG vs. NCL) between single-run observations and 100-seed means.#

Solution: Identified that the single-run observation was an ‘unlucky draw’, whereas the 100-seed mean correctly reflects the method’s stability.

7. Silent fallback from mclust to KMeans was masking potential clustering algorithm biases.#

Solution: Developed and ran a ‘fallback audit’ (mclust_fallback_audit.py) to check the stability of the clusterer across seeds.

General Issues#

8. Slow transfer speed (approx 245 KB/s) for the 11.5GB checkpoint via single scp.#

Learnings#

Key Learnings#

  • Precise control over quantization scales in sensitive layers (like RMSNorm) is critical; noise must be significantly lower than the L2 distance between fine-grained actions.
  • Fusion gains in spatial omics are non-monotonic across scales: they augment tissue-level organization but can degrade fine-grained cell-type resolution.
  • A unified multitask VLA combined with a visual-memory prompting framework provides a much stronger research claim than task-specific VLAs.
  • TF-Enhanced calibration can significantly improve the effective range of 4-bit quantization without increasing bit-width or altering graph structure.
  • When syncing data across devices, the ‘provider’ tag is critical for bucket identity; mismatched tags lead to incorrect aggregation.
