Learnings#

Key Learnings#

  • Constrained writing (asking narrow, specific questions) significantly outperforms free-form writing in VLM-driven visual prompting (53/65 vs 39/65 successes).
  • When describing research, focus on the ‘bottleneck’ and the ‘reasoning’ behind a solution rather than just listing high-performance metrics.
  • In PhD applications, distinguish between a model’s failure to generalize and a fundamental change in the environment’s task state.
  • Task queue priority is critical in agentic workflows to prevent high-latency tasks (like summarization) from blocking time-sensitive media processing.
  • Using a ‘contact sheet’ approach for animated stickers is an effective way to support LLMs that only accept single-image inputs.
