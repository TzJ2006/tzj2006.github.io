Problems & Solutions#

Critical Issues#

1. LLM misinterpreting text in images and losing clarity in long screenshots due to scaling.#

Solution: Decoupled text extraction from semantic interpretation by using a segmented reading approach with a local CPU OCR engine.

Key Insight: For multimodal assistants, separating ‘what is written’ (OCR) from ‘what it means’ (LLM) significantly reduces hallucinations.

2. The SOP narrative for ‘Latent Bridge’ lacked a cohesive research arc and sounded like a technical mismatch.#

Solution: Rewrote the section to bridge ‘what the method does’ with ’the lesson learned’ regarding system vs. policy errors, using ‘recovery thresholds’ as a unifying theme.

Key Insight: Connecting individual research projects through shared themes creates a stronger narrative arc than presenting them as isolated successes.

3. OCR misidentifying symbols (e.g., ‘?’ as ‘2’) and testing risks interfering with live services.#

Solution: Implemented an uncertainty label ‘[疑似符号，需核对]’ for low-confidence characters and created a ’test-only’ mode that pauses receiving services.

Key Insight: Building a ’test-only’ mode that can safely pause the primary service is crucial to prevent race conditions.

4. Manuscript title and results overclaimed ‘agent’ impact and used unproven metrics.#

Solution: Retitled to focus on benchmarking and capability; moved agent harness to Methods to align with Nature Methods’ preference for evidence-based writing.

Key Insight: Nature Methods authors prefer ‘capability’ (what it can do) over ‘automation’ (how it was done).

General Issues#

5. Managing a large workload for recommenders and technical bugs like sticker domain blocking.#

Solution: Added considerate language to emails to ease workload perception and updated the domain allowlist for WeChat sticker CDNs.
